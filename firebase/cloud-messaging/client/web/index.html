📖 How to Use

  1. Request Permission: Click "Request Permission" and allow notifications in your browser.
  2. Get Token: Click "Get Token" to generate your FCM token.
  3. Register Token: Enter your User ID and Device ID, then click "Register Token".
  4. Send Notification: Fill in the notification details and click "Send".
  5. Schedule Notification: Set a future date/time and schedule a notification for later delivery.
  6. View History: Check your notification history and statistics.
  7. Test Background: Minimize or switch tabs, then send a notification via Swagger UI.
